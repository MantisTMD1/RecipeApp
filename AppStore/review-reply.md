The app has no accounts, no user-generated content and no paid content or features.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Recipe Rescue helps home cooks decide what to make with the ingredients they already have. Users add the items in their kitchen, and the app suggests recipes ranked by how few ingredients are missing, then builds a shopping list for anything they still need. It is aimed at everyday home cooks who want to reduce food waste and save time planning meals. The app is free, has no ads and collects no personal data.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No sign-in or setup is required. An internet connection is needed to load recipes.
- Home tab: a featured recipe, categories, new recipes and random suggestions. Tap the shuffle button for new ideas.
- Kitchen tab: tap +, search for and select ingredients (for example Chicken, Garlic and Onion), tap Done, then tap "What Can I Make?".
- On any recipe: ingredients you have are checked off; tap "Add Missing to Shopping List".
- List tab: check off items while shopping, then use the menu to move them to your kitchen.
- Favorites tab and Search are also available.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- TheMealDB (https://www.themealdb.com): recipe data and images, accessed through its API with a paid supporter (production) key. This is the only external service. There is no authentication service, payment processor, analytics, advertising or AI service. All user data (kitchen, favorites, shopping list) is stored only on the device.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app functions identically in all regions. The content is in English.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Recipe content and images come from TheMealDB. I am a TheMealDB supporter, which TheMealDB requires for apps released on app stores, and the app uses the production API key issued to me. The app credits TheMealDB in its description and in Settings.

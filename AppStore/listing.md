## Promotional text (170 max)

Not sure what's for dinner? Tell Recipe Rescuer what's in your kitchen and get recipes you can make tonight, plus a shopping list for anything you're missing.

## Description (4,000 max)

Recipe Rescuer turns the ingredients you already have into dinner ideas.

COOK WITH WHAT YOU HAVE
Tap what's in your kitchen and hit "What Can I Make?". Recipes are ranked by how few ingredients you're missing, so you can see at a glance what's ready to cook and what needs one quick shop.

STEP-BY-STEP COOKING MODE
Big, easy-to-read steps one at a time, with the screen kept awake. Any time a step mentions ("simmer for 20 minutes") becomes a one-tap timer that alerts you even when your phone is locked.

YOUR OWN RECIPES
Save family favorites with a photo, ingredients and steps. They work everywhere: cooking mode, the shopping list and recipe ideas.

MAKE MORE OR LESS
Halve, double or triple any recipe and every amount updates, including your shopping list.

EAT THE WAY YOU EAT
Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and dairy-free filters apply across the whole app.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
Put recipes on the days you'll cook them, then add everything you're missing to your shopping list in one tap.

SHOP SMARTER
Add a recipe's missing ingredients to your shopping list in one tap. Check items off as you shop, then move them into your kitchen when you get home.

DISCOVER SOMETHING NEW
Browse by cuisine or by breakfast, lunch, dinner and dessert, see estimated cooking times and calories, and get a fresh feed of recipes from around the world every time you open the app.

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
Your kitchen, saved recipes, shopping list and meal plan stay up to date through your own iCloud account.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your devices and in your private iCloud, where we can't see it.

Recipe data from TheMealDB. Nutrition estimates use USDA FoodData Central.

## App Review notes

No sign-in is required. The app loads recipe data from TheMealDB's public API, so the reviewer's device needs an internet connection. To try the main feature: open the Kitchen tab, add a few ingredients such as Chicken, Garlic and Onion, then tap "What Can I Make?".

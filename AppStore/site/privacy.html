Privacy Policy

Recipe Rescue · Last updated October 10, 2026

Recipe Rescue is built to work without collecting anything about you. This policy explains what the app stores and what it sends over the internet.

Information we collect

None. Recipe Rescue has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your favorites, the ingredients you add to your kitchen, your shopping list and your settings are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Recipe data

To show recipes, the app requests them from TheMealDB, a third-party recipe database. These requests contain only what is needed to fetch recipes, such as a search term, a category or an ingredient name. Like any website, TheMealDB's servers may receive your device's IP address when the app connects. See TheMealDB's own terms for how it handles requests.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

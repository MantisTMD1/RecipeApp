Common questions

Why don't I see any recipes? Recipe Rescue needs an internet connection to load recipes. Check your connection and pull down to refresh.

How are recipe ideas chosen? The app looks up recipes that use the ingredients in your kitchen and ranks them by how few ingredients you're missing. You can turn "Assume Pantry Staples" on or off in Settings.

Is my data backed up? Your kitchen, favorites and shopping list are stored only on your device.
